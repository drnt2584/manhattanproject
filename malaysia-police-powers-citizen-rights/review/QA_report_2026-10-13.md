## Overall answer

The reviewer's substantive legal verdicts on Assertions, Rules, Entities and Routes are acceptable: no approved claim was found to misstate the cited provision. The review is not acceptable for sign-off as it stands, for three reasons.

1. The Sources & currency tab does not answer the question its columns ask. "Amendments checked up to (date)" was filled with "Reprint date - confirmed" or with reprint and gazette dates. No row states the date up to which later amendments were checked, or whether any cited section was affected by the amending Acts the reviewer lists. One date is wrong on the supplied material (Act 846 commencement) and one statement is wrong (an "Oct 2026" CMA amendment; the October 2026 Act supplied amends Act 589, not Act 588).
2. Several "verified against official reprint p.X" notes on the Provisions tab cite pages that do not contain the section. Five page references are wrong and one consolidation (CPC s.15) was "verified" against a reprint that predates the amendment. This shows the page checks the Read me required were not done for every row.
3. Three of the four Calculator open questions were not answered. The replies restate the remand caps or assert a rule ("a part-day counts as a full day") with no authority. All Calculator replies contain bracketed citations [1] to [7] that point to no reference list.

A fourth issue needs a decision rather than a correction: 16 Assertions and 4 Entities were marked Amend solely for second-person wording. The Read me's standard is "information rather than instruction"; it does not require third person. The same standard was not applied to the Rules tab, where all 31 app texts are second person and all were Approved. Either adopt a third-person house style and apply it to every tab, or withdraw the 20 Amends.

## Sources & currency

The column "Amendments checked up to (date)" requires a date and a result. Every principal-Act row instead says "Reprint date - confirmed" (or a reprint date), which confirms the pack's own version and nothing more. Specific problems:

- Act 846 (two rows): commencement given as 16/6/2023. The Penal Code reprint supplied states Act 846 came into operation on 4 July 2023. 16 June 2023 is the Gazette publication date.
- CMA 1998: "latest Oct 2026" is unsupported. The October 2026 Act in the project (A1799, Royal Assent 25 Sep 2026) amends the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission Act (Act 589), not the CMA (Act 588).
- A1743 (CMA amendment 2025): the currency note asked for the commencement date for s.249 specifically, because the Act may commence in parts. A single date was given and the s.249 "In force" cell still reads "not confirmed".
- SOSMA: "Act 747 NOT the one declared in the currency note" is unintelligible. Act 747 is SOSMA; the currency note names A1736.
- A1736 and A1732: identical dates (01/11/2024 and 03/03/2025) for two different Acts. Confirm this is not a copy-paste.
- Later amending Acts for the CPC (A1682, A1722), Penal Code (A1691, A1721, A1750) and Evidence Act (A1732) are listed with dates but with no statement of which sections they amend. The pack cites CPC ss.15-28A, 56, 64-65, 111-117; Penal Code ss.330, 339-342, 348; Evidence Act ss.24-26. Whether any of these changed is the whole point of the column.
- RTA "reprint 15/10/2023", MACC "reprint 1/1/2022", A1794 "15/09/2026 not yet in force": cannot be checked against supplied sources; the reviewer should add the LOM page reference for each.
- "Reprint date" is the wrong label for amending Acts; those are Gazette publication dates.
- "Checked by": name and method are fine; add the date of the check and use past tense.
